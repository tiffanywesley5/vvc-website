About the Founder

I build things that work while I rest.

And now I build them for other founders too.

Tiffany Snow

I am a full-stack developer, AI animator and production engineer, an award-winning beauty founder, and a serial entrepreneur. I spent seven years at Block (formerly Square) in managerial roles including Beta Manager for the Square Card program. I graduated from Savvy Coders for full-stack development and Composition Media's AI animation and production class, the first program of its kind in St. Louis. I also hold an active esthetician license and have been building businesses from the ground up for as long as I can remember.

I started Velvet Vision Creative because I kept seeing the same problem. Founders, especially in beauty, wellness, and the creative industries, were doing the work of five people with the budget of half a person. They needed systems, not just tools. They needed infrastructure that works even when they are not working.

That is what we build. AI agent systems that answer your customer questions at midnight. AI animation and production for brands that need dynamic visual content. Content engines that draft your social media while you focus on clients. Research agents that keep you informed about your industry without hours of scrolling. And we teach you how every piece works so you own the system, not just rent it.

What I Believe

Ownership Matters

You should own your systems, not rent them. Every agent we build, every line of code, every document belongs to you. No vendor lock-in. No black boxes.

Multipassionate Wins

Being good at multiple things is not a weakness. It is a compounding advantage. VVC exists because beauty, technology, and business strategy are not separate worlds. They are one.
